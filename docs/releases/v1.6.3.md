# v1.6.3 · 双层圆环、常驻数字与灰白紫界面

任务栏与宠物挂件同时显示剩余额度和到下班可用，挂件内只有双层圆环与数字，无需点击查看。详情、设置和提示框统一采用背景 `#EFF1F5`、文字 `#4C4F69`、强调色 `#8839EF`。

## 更新内容

- **外圈 / 上方数字**：实际剩余额度。**内圈 / 下方数字**：到下班可用，等于实际剩余减去原有下班目标，以整周额度的百分点显示。
- 低于目标时，下方显示负数并使用警示色，内圈清空；数据延迟时余额显示 `≈`，可用余量显示 `—`；未知额度不会误显示成 0。
- 宠物挂件默认 100 × 40 DIP，任务栏约 90 × 36 DIP，随缩放调整。保留悬停说明、点击详情、右键任务栏设置、宠物拖动及雷达提醒圆点。
- 统一浅色面板、圆角按钮和提示框。保留作息、额度算法、历史、手动导入、中英文和通知；沿用 v1.6.2 的 Windows 11 任务栏绘制修复。

## English

Both display modes now show dual rings and two numeric values without text labels: outer/top is quota remaining; inner/bottom is headroom until close, calculated from the existing schedule and closing target. Deficits show a negative value with an empty inner arc. Stale balances use `≈` without a current budget; unavailable data uses `—` instead of zero.

The Pet widget is 100 × 40 DIP; the taskbar widget is approximately 90 × 36 DIP. Details, settings, charts and tooltips share background `#EFF1F5`, text `#4C4F69` and accent `#8839EF`. Dragging, clicks, radar alerts, saved schedules, calculations, history, imports and both languages remain available. The Windows 11 layered-rendering fix is retained.

Validated on Windows 11 26H2 build 26300.9711 at 125% scaling: 366 pacing/model/UI, 476 language/persistence, 46 taskbar, 22 reset-signal and 15 overlay assertions, plus 1,120 layout cases and drag/usage compatibility checks. Offscreen ring rendering covers 100%–200% DPI. Other native DPI settings, builds, Explorer restarts and auto-hide need separate validation; Windows 10 was not retested.

Exit the previous app, extract the entire archive with its DLL files and update your shortcut. Existing settings and history are retained. The Windows x64 build is unsigned; verify it with `SHA256SUMS.txt` from this release.
